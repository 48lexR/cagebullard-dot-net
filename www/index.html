Welcome to Cagebullard.net!

Cagebullard.net is the personal website of Micajah Bullard, a senior at the University of North Carolina - Chapel Hill.

Here, I believe that

  1. less is more,
  2. there's no need to use a 3-cent word when a 2-cent word will do instead,
  3. and you can't get to heaven in a red canoe.

I am particularly interested in the internet and its architecture including protocols, applications, and serivces. I am also interested in operating systems and distributed systems. Some projects that I've worked on recently include

  • TCP Tar Heee: a TCP congestion control algorithm,
  • COMP 560 Final Project: a model to beat a modified version of the game quorodors, and
  • C++ SMTP Server: a C++ project that implements RFC 821.

When I'm not programming, I'm performing with the UNC Marching Tar Heels, analyzing Carolina football, writing, or playing the violin.
