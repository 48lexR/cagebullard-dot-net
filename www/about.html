About me

A long long time ago (actually, an int16_t time ago, if you get my joke) I was (a Tar Heel) born! My mom and dad were both Tar Heels, themselves, making me a Tar Heel bred. Given that I haven't yet died, I'm not yet a Tar Heel dead.

I grew up in Southeastern North Carolina, where I went to High School amidst the corn and tobacco fields. I grew up playing the violin and banjo, as well as playing mellophone and tuba in my high school marching band. My love for programming and computers was born when I stumbled across the Kahn Academy ProcessingJS tutorial.

I graduated from the North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics, where I met my boyfriend and further developed my love for programming. While there, I maintained my love of music, even being first chair all-state French Horn my senior year, 2023. (I even earned the honor to play the solo from John Williams' Princess Leia's Theme from Star Wars!)

And, like everywhere else, at Carolina, I nurtured my love for computers and my love for music, as well as my love of language by adding a Chinese major and a linguistics minor. Like many that graduate from this place, I am a rabid fan of all things Carolina athletics. I was fortunate enough to go to Ireland and perform with the band for the Tar Heels' 15-10 revenge victory over TCU in 2026.

Currently, I'm an intern at Triangle E-Cycling, a local business aimed at keeping used hardware in use; Vaughan Law Firms, where I am an IT consultant; and Carolina Performing Arts, where I am both an usher and a head usher.

I spend a lot of time honing my programming skills. Most often, I use C, C++, Go, Python and JavaScript. I have plenty of experience with Docker including tools like compose and run. I have been very fortunate to be surrounded by excellent faculty such as Dr. Kevin Jeffay, Dr. Kaki Ryan, and Prof. Ben Berg.

As it relates to computers, my greatest interest is in computer networking. The internet is a huge, near-ubiquitous piece of technology that most of us never think about. When I started homelabbing in December of 2025, I realized how interested I was in all of its concepts. Now, I'm working toward my Cisco CNA certificate.

Of all the various inspirations in my life, former UNC men's basketball head coach Roy Williams perhaps one of my greatest.

It's amazing what we can accomplish when nobody cares who gets the credit.

- Roy Williams, Hard Work (2010)
